\section{Purpose}

This document defines the cryptographic requirements for SendCUIEmail to ensure compliance with federal standards for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). These requirements establish the minimum security controls that must be implemented and verified.

% ============================================================================
% SCOPE
% ============================================================================
\section{Scope}

These requirements apply to:
\begin{itemize}[topsep=4pt, itemsep=2pt]
    \item File encryption and decryption operations
    \item Password/key derivation functions
    \item Random number generation for cryptographic material
    \item All platforms where SendCUIEmail operates (Windows, macOS, Linux)
\end{itemize}

REQ-1.1 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) as defined in FIPS 197 for symmetric encryption. \\[6pt]

REQ-1.2 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use a key length of 256 bits (AES-256) for all encryption operations. \\[6pt]

REQ-1.3 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use Cipher Block Chaining (CBC) mode or an authenticated encryption mode (GCM, CCM). \\[6pt]

REQ-1.4 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL generate a unique, random Initialization Vector (IV) for each encryption operation. \\[6pt]

REQ-1.5 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The IV SHALL be at least 128 bits (16 bytes) for AES-CBC mode. \\[6pt]

REQ-1.6 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The IV SHALL be stored with or transmitted alongside the ciphertext. \\[6pt]

\bottomrule

REQ-2.1 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use PBKDF2 (Password-Based Key Derivation Function 2) as defined in NIST SP 800-132 for deriving encryption keys from passwords. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.2 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use HMAC-SHA256 as the pseudorandom function (PRF) for PBKDF2. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.3 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL use a minimum of 100,000 iterations for PBKDF2 per NIST SP 800-132 recommendations. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.4 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The system SHALL generate a unique, random salt for each encryption operation. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.5 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The salt SHALL be at least 128 bits (16 bytes) as recommended by NIST SP 800-132. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.6 & \textcolor{mandatory}{\textbf{[M]}} The salt SHALL be stored with or transmitted alongside the ciphertext. \\[6pt]

REQ-2.7 & \textcolor{recommended}{\textbf{[R]}} The iteration count SHOULD be increased over time as computing power increases. \\[6pt]
